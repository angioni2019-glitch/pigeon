Sign in to Telegram for your watch test servers

Connecting to Telegram…

Telegram sends a login code to your Telegram app.

Scan it with Telegram on another phone or tablet. No code needed.

Never share this code with anyone.

On another phone or tablet signed in to Telegram, open Settings › Devices › Link Desktop Device and scan this code. It refreshes every 30 seconds.

Only have this phone? Try opening it in Telegram. Telegram may refuse this for safety; scanning always works.

Sending the login to your watch…

Open this page from your watch: in Pigeon Lab, choose Link account, then open the Garmin Connect notification on your phone.

This page isn’t set up yet: put your Telegram api_id and api_hash in config.js.

Pigeon is an unofficial app that uses the Telegram API. This page talks only to Telegram: it creates your watch’s login key, encrypts it so only your watch can read it, and keeps nothing.
